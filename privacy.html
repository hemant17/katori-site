Katori Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Katori helps you track what you eat, how you move and how you sleep. Health data is personal; this policy explains what Katori collects, where it lives and how you stay in control.

Katori is published by Hemant Bhardwaj, an individual developer in India, who decides how the data described here is used. Contact: hemantbhardwaj17@gmail.com.

What we collect

Data Why Where it is stored
Profile: name, sex, birth date, height, weight, goal, diet preference, region, health conditions you choose to share To calculate your calorie and nutrient targets On your phone. In the Katori cloud only if you create an account
Food, water, weight, workout and sleep logs To show your day, trends and your Tomorrow plan On your phone. In the Katori cloud only if you create an account
Meal photos you take with Snap To recognise the food on your plate Sent to our AI service for recognition. Stored on your phone; never used to train models
Voice you record with Say it Converted to text by your phone's built-in speech recognition (on Android and iPhone this may be handled by Google or Apple under your device's settings and their privacy policies) Katori never receives or stores the audio. Only the resulting text is sent to our servers for parsing
Body photos for Future You (optional) To create a projected image of you Your photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI service for the one-time edit and is not kept on our servers; your original also stays on your phone. The generated image is stored privately in your account and can be deleted at any time
Wearable data via Health Connect / Apple Health: steps, active energy, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, weight, workouts To adapt your plan to how rested and active you are Read on your phone; daily summaries are stored on your phone. Not sold, not shared
Data Katori writes to Health Connect / Apple Health: meals (nutrition), water, weight, workouts So your other health apps see what you log in Katori In Health Connect / Apple Health under your control
Account: email address To sign you in and back up your data Katori cloud

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We never share health data with insurers, employers or data brokers.
  • Data read from Health Connect or Apple Health is used only to provide Katori features to you, in line with the Health Connect and HealthKit policies.

AI processing

Snap, Say it, the Katori coach, plan notes and Future You use large language and image models through our backend. Requests are sent over encrypted connections and tied to your account for rate limiting. We ask our AI gateway to use only providers that do not store prompts or images and do not train models on them; our servers do not keep your meal photos or original body photos. Katori gives general wellness guidance and is not a medical device.

If you report an AI answer or image from inside the app, we receive the text you reported, any note you add and your account ID so we can review it. Reports are deleted with your account.

Your controls

  • Export: Settings, Privacy, Export my data (JSON).
  • Delete on device: Settings, Privacy, Delete all data on this phone.
  • Delete account: Settings, Privacy, Delete my account removes your cloud data within 30 days (backups within 35 days).
  • Wearables: disconnect any time in Settings, Wearables or in the Health Connect app.
  • Permissions: camera, microphone, notifications and health permissions are requested only when you use the feature that needs them.

Children

Katori is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Laws

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, where applicable, GDPR and US state consumer health data laws (e.g. Washington's My Health My Data Act). Contact: hemantbhardwaj17@gmail.com.
