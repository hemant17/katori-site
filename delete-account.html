Delete your Katori account

Katori, an app by Hemant Bhardwaj.

This page explains how to delete your Katori account and everything backed up with it, and what happens afterwards. You do not need this page to delete your account — the fastest way is in the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Settings — open Settings from your profile tab.
  2. Privacy — scroll to the Privacy section.
  3. Delete my account — confirm, and your cloud data is removed within 30 days (backups within 35 days).

Cannot open the app? Email us

Send one line to hemantbhardwaj17@gmail.com from the email address tied to your Katori account, asking us to delete it. We reply to confirm, and your cloud data is removed within 30 days (backups within 35 days).

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, sex, birth date, height, weight, goal, diet preference, region, health conditions you shared.
  • All food, water, weight, workout and sleep logs.
  • Coach messages and your plans, including Tomorrow plans.
  • Custom foods and recipes you created.
  • Cloud-stored Future You images and photos.

Deleted means deleted: we do not keep a copy for ourselves.

What stays on your phone

Data that never left your device — meals logged offline, meal photos, your original Future You photo — stays on the phone until you delete it in the app (Settings, Privacy, Delete all data on this phone) or uninstall Katori.

Billing

We do not keep billing records. Payments and subscriptions are handled by Google Play, so any payment records live in your Google account, not with us.

Changed your mind? Just keep using the app — nothing is deleted until you confirm.
