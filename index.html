Your day as a thali

Four katoris tell you what your plate is missing

See calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber fill up like katoris on a plate. Water, steps, sleep, weight and workouts sit right beside your meals.

  • Protein fills the anaar katori — dal, paneer, eggs, chicken.
  • Carbs fill the haldi katori — roti, rice, poha, upma.
  • Fat fills the sarson katori — ghee, oil, nuts, seeds.
  • Fiber fills the pudina katori — sabzi, fruit, whole grains.

The katori in the middle is your energy for the day, in kcal.

A plan for tomorrow

A plan that adapts to your sleep, not a strict menu

Slept badly? Katori swaps the heavy workout for a lighter one and nudges your protein up. Big wedding dinner? It spreads the extra over the next few days instead of making you starve.

Meal ideas match your diet: veg, eggetarian, non-veg, vegan or Jain. Your targets follow the ICMR–NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians.

  • Slept 5 hours? Tomorrow starts lighter, with an earlier bedtime suggestion.
  • Trained hard? Protein goes up and rest is planned in.
  • Over by 300 kcal? The week rebalances gently over the next few days.
  • Forgot to drink water? A calm nudge, not a scolding.

Where our food data comes from

Numbers you can trace

Every nutrient value in Katori carries its source. This is the attribution we show in the app:

Food data sources. Nutrient values come from the Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB), Anuvaad Solutions (Vijayakumar A, Dubasi HB, Awasthi A, Jaacks LM. Development of an Indian Food Composition Database. Current Developments in Nutrition 2024;8(7):103790, doi:10.1016/j.cdnut.2024.103790), whose recipe values are derived from the ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition Indian Food Composition Tables 2017 (Longvah T, Ananthan R, Bhaskarachary K, Venkaiah K) and Nutritive Value of Indian Foods (2004), the UK McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset (2021) and USDA FoodData Central; and from USDA FoodData Central (U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service; SR Legacy, FNDDS 2021–2023 and Branded Foods; fdc.nal.usda.gov; public domain). Some dishes are Katori recipe calculations from these sources, and deep-fried INDB recipes are adjusted for frying oil that is not absorbed. Portion sizes are typical household estimates. Values are approximate and for general information only; they are not medical advice. Brand names belong to their owners.

Packaged foods are looked up live in Open Food Facts, whose open data is published under the Open Database Licence (ODbL).

Questions

Frequently asked

Does Katori work offline?

Yes. Your data lives on your phone, so logging, the thali and your plan work without a network. Sign-in, cloud backup and AI features need a connection.

Which foods are in the database?

More than 1,500 dishes and ingredients, from idli sambar to moth chawal. Values come from the Indian Nutrient Databank and USDA FoodData Central, and you can search in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

Does it work with my wearable?

On Android, Katori connects through Health Connect, so steps, sleep, heart rate and workouts from Fitbit, Garmin, Oura, Samsung Health and more come in automatically.

Is Katori a medical device?

No. Katori gives general wellness guidance and is not a medical device. If you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medicines such as insulin, GLP-1 or thyroid medication, follow your doctor's advice.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Settings, then Privacy, then Delete my account. You can also request deletion by email — see the delete account page.
